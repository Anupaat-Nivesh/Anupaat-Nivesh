## Content Strategy Recommendations

### 1. **Left Side Content (Hero Content)**
**Principle:** Keep it concise, scannable, and action-oriented.

#### ✅ Current Approach (Good):
- **Eyebrow:** Credibility indicator (AMFI Registered)
- **Heading:** Clear value proposition
- **Benefit Line:** One-line value statement (NEW)
- **CTA:** Clear action button
- **Pills:** Quick trust indicators

#### 💡 Recommendations:
- **Benefit Line:** Should be 12-15 words max, focusing on the primary outcome
  - Screen 1: "Expert guidance for goal-based investing with disciplined SIPs and regular portfolio reviews."
  - Screen 2: "Get personalized financial planning, investment strategies, and goal achievement roadmap in one session."
- **Pills:** Keep to 3 items max, use checkmarks for quick scanning
- **No long paragraphs:** Users scan, not read in hero sections

---

### 2. **Right Side Content (Hero Panel)**
**Principle:** Show credibility, specificity, and value through numbers and details.

#### ✅ Current Approach (Good):
- **Badge:** Context setting
- **Stats Grid:** 2x2 layout with labels, values, hints
- **Note:** Additional value prop or testimonial snippet

#### 💡 Recommendations:

**Screen 1 Panel Enhancements:**
- **Stats:** Current 4 stats are good, but consider:
  - Add a 5th stat if space allows (e.g., "Portfolio Reviews/Year: 4+")
  - Make hints more benefit-focused (e.g., "Growing your wealth systematically" instead of "Disciplined, goal-aligned")
- **Note Section:** Could be:
  - A short testimonial quote (1 line)
  - A specific benefit (e.g., "Track your investments 24/7 with our digital platform")
  - A guarantee/assurance (e.g., "No hidden fees, transparent advisory")

**Screen 2 Panel Enhancements:**
- **Stats:** Current structure works well
- **Note Section:** Should emphasize urgency or value:
  - "Limited slots available this month"
  - "Includes post-session action plan & resources"
  - "100% money-back guarantee if not satisfied"

## Content Length Guidelines

### Left Side (Hero Content):
- **Eyebrow:** 3-5 words
- **Heading:** 6-10 words (can be split across 2 lines)
- **Benefit Line:** 12-15 words
- **CTA Button:** 2-5 words
- **Pills:** 2-4 words each, 3 pills max

### Right Side (Hero Panel):
- **Badge:** 3-6 words
- **Stat Labels:** 2-4 words
- **Stat Values:** Numbers or short phrases (e.g., "30 Minutes", "₹25 Cr+")
- **Stat Hints:** 4-8 words
- **Note:** 12-20 words (1-2 sentences)

---

## Content Writing Principles

### 1. **Outcome-Focused**
- Focus on what the user gets, not what you do
- ❌ "We provide financial advisory services"
- ✅ "Build long-term wealth through clarity & discipline"

### 2. **Specific Over Generic**
- Use numbers, timeframes, and concrete details
- ❌ "Expert guidance"
- ✅ "8+ years of advisory experience, ₹25 Cr+ AUM managed"

### 3. **Benefit-Driven**
- Every element should answer "What's in it for me?"
- Pills should highlight benefits, not features
- Stats should show credibility and results

### 4. **Action-Oriented**
- CTAs should be clear and specific
- Use action verbs: "Get", "Build", "Start", "Book"
- Avoid passive language

### 5. **Scannable**
- Use short sentences
- Break up text with visual elements (pills, badges, stats)
- Use bullet points or checkmarks for lists
